INSTRUMENT BANKS, FOR SONGKIT

SONGKIT's bank page (Ctrl+B) lists the banks in this folder, plays their
instruments on the keyboard, and takes them into a song. A bank is a WOPL
file (.WOP): the format of Wohlstand's OPL3 Bank Editor and libADLMIDI, which
can make WOPL files from many other OPL banks. Put more of them here, or make
your own with Ctrl+N on the bank page and fill it with Ctrl+P.

FATMAN2.WOP   The Fat Man's General MIDI bank, 2 operators
FATMAN4.WOP   The Fat Man's General MIDI bank, 4 operators (OPL3)

Each has a melodic bank (the 128 General MIDI programs) and a percussion
bank (the General MIDI drums, on keys 35-81). They are The Fat Man's
fatman-2op.wopl and fatman-4op.wopl, from
https://github.com/Wohlstand/TheFatManOPL, with the General MIDI names
added (they come with none) by gamekit/tools/wopl_names.py.
